# Product Vision

## Product statement

NovelForge is an author-controlled platform for creating and maintaining
very long webnovels with AI assistance without surrendering canon,
continuity, planning, or recovery to the model.

## Primary user jobs

-   Plan a novel from premise through ending.
-   Write/edit chapters with inline and agent-assisted AI.
-   Maintain canon, characters, relationships, world rules, timeline,
    and unresolved threads.
-   Support dynamic outlines that evolve from actual story consequences.
-   Handle 2,000--2,500+ chapter projects without injecting the full
    manuscript into prompts.
-   Model optional story systems such as romance/harem/polygamy,
    civilization building, technology progression, politics, historical
    divergence, war-state consequences, and reputation.
-   Control drama frequency and narrative rhythm without mechanical
    repetition.
-   Reach an earned ending with tracked resolution debt.
-   Safely experiment, branch, checkpoint, compare, and restore story
    state.
-   Publish explicit chapter versions and let non-member readers discover,
    navigate and follow the released story without exposing drafts or spoilers.
-   Import/export retained formats and manage AI definitions, routing,
    references, usage and evaluation through usable author/admin surfaces.

## Delivery success

Prove the complete author loop: approved outline → generated/reviewed chapter
→ author decisions → versioned manuscript and approved state → fresh memory
for the next chapter. Prove recovery and the public reader journey separately.
Backend phase completion alone does not establish these outcomes. Retained
original requirements and release cuts are tracked in
[DELIVERY-PLAN](../implementation/DELIVERY-PLAN.md).

## Product pillars

1.  Author sovereignty
2.  Structured story truth
3.  Long-form continuity
4.  Consequence-aware progression
5.  Provider-independent AI orchestration
6.  Evidence-backed diagnostics
7.  Safe experimentation and recovery
8.  Progressive disclosure in both UI and LLM context

## Example supported novel

A modern genius reincarnates 500--600 years into the past in a poor
body, applies bounded modern knowledge, improves a village, builds
reputation among scholars and political institutions, participates in
wars and state-building, and eventually becomes emperor. The novel may
contain multiple spouses/harem relationships, temporary antagonists,
enemy-to-ally arcs, long calm intervals, and a triumphant/nostalgic
ending around 2,500 chapters.

This example is a validation scenario, not a hardcoded genre.
