Chapter 85 · Scene 1

Ember Debt

The bargaining hall·Draft·1,412 words

The hall smelled of wet iron and old smoke. Veyra counted the braziers out of habit — seven, same as the night her father burned the ledgers — and found the number wanting.

“You ask for a tenth of the northern pass,” Kael said, and his voice carried the tired arithmetic of a man who had already lost this argument in three futures. “The pass does not grow embers, Veyra. Men do.”

She let the silence do its work. Outside, the wind dragged its nails down the Ash Gate, and somewhere in the upper city a bell tolled once, then thought better of it.

“Men burn,” she said at last. “Embers remember.”
