# Story Simulation Specification

## Goal

Represent persistent consequences without attempting a perfect
real-world simulator. Modules are optional and activated per novel.

Delivery dependency: complete the M1–M3 chapter/recovery/memory loop first,
then the branch/recovery support required by each M6 module. Uncertain causal
effects are proposals with evidence, not automatic canon. Use the shared
Story Transaction and revision-derived projections; no separate unchecked
simulation write path. What-If must remain isolated from main until an
authorized, compatibility-checked merge.

## Generic primitives

Entity, State, Event, Relationship, Knowledge, Resource, Rule,
Dependency, Cause, Effect, Territory, Organization, Reputation,
Timeline.

Genre/story modules compose these primitives.

## Civilization

Track appropriate state for settlements/regions: population, food
security, infrastructure, education, crafts/industry, commerce,
governance, security, migration, public order, and author-defined
dimensions.

## Technology/innovation

Knowing an idea does not equal production capability.

An innovation may require: knowledge, prerequisite technologies,
materials, tools, precision, skills, energy, capital, supply chain,
time, prototypes, iteration.

Track invention, production readiness, adoption, diffusion, affected
stakeholders, benefits, costs, opposition, unintended consequences.

## Information diffusion and reputation

Knowledge/reputation has scope and propagation delay. Store evidence and
reach by settlement, institution, faction, region, or audience.

## Economy/politics

Meaningful innovation can create winners, losers, price/supply changes,
institutional resistance, new revenue, migration, political influence,
or instability. Do not auto-generate consequences without
evidence/rules; generate proposals when uncertain.

## Historical divergence

For reincarnation/regression: - original historical expectation; -
current altered history; - divergence causes; - future-knowledge
reliability: reliable / partially diverged / highly diverged /
unreliable.

## Conflict/war narrative state

Track belligerents, objectives, territory, commanders, force condition,
morale, supply condition, political support, civilian/economic effects,
alliances, intelligence state, major engagements, and consequences.

This module supports fictional narrative state and strategic
plausibility. It must not be designed as a repository of actionable
real-world weapon construction instructions.

## Opponent learning

Observed strategies/technologies can update opponent knowledge and
countermeasures. Reusing an already-public surprise tactic should
trigger a plausibility warning unless justified.
