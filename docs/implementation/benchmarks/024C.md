## 1. Query/latency optimization battery (contract Part 2)

### Investigation

Reviewed the 023B pinned workload results (`docs/implementation/023B-budget-table.md`):

| Route | p95 | Budget | Avg queries |
|---|---|---|---|
| GET novel | 4ms | 200ms | ~3 |
| GET chapter | 5ms | 200ms | ~4 |
| PUT chapter | 6ms | 500ms | ~4 |

**Finding:** 9/9 routes well within budget (worst case: 21ms vs 2000ms budget = 1% of budget).
Average query counts: 3–8 per request. No N+1 patterns observed. The merge
preview path (`computeMergePreview`) already parallelizes its three
`Promise.all` batches (change sets, snapshots, head revisions).

### Conclusion

No safe ≥15% optimization opportunity exists on the pinned workload that
does not risk observable behavior change (024 Invariant 0). Forcing an
optimization to meet the threshold would violate "without weaker invariants"
— the governing constraint of 024.
